Features built for the shop floor

Every tool StockSync offers exists to answer one question: what do we actually have in stock?

01

Real-time stock tracking

Every sale, return, and transfer updates your stock count instantly across all devices and registers.

02

Barcode scanning & labelling

Scan items in and out with any phone camera or handheld scanner, and print labels straight from the dashboard.

03

Low-stock alerts

Set a reorder threshold per product and get an email or SMS alert before you run out.

04

Multi-store sync

Run two shops or twenty. Every location shares one live view of stock, with easy stock transfers between stores.

05

Supplier & purchase orders

Generate purchase orders from your low-stock list and send them straight to your suppliers.

06

Sales reports & analytics

See your best sellers, slow movers, and stock value at a glance, exportable for your accountant.

How it works

From delivery to the shelf in three steps

This is a design prototype only. In the full product, each step below is powered by live data.

1

Scan it in

Scan new stock as it arrives so counts are accurate from day one.

2

Sell it

Every sale at the till automatically reduces the stock count in real time.

3

Reorder it

StockSync flags low stock and drafts a purchase order before you sell out.
