About StockSync

We started StockSync because counting stock by hand shouldn't be part of anyone's job description.

Our story

Built by people who worked the shop floor

StockSync began in 2025 after our founders Bijaya Sharma, Adesh Pokhrel and Arpana Adhikari spent a weekend doing a full manual stocktake at a friend's homeware store. Three spreadsheets and one very late night later, we decided retailers deserved something better.

Today, StockSync gives independent shops the same real-time inventory tools that big chains use, without the enterprise price tag or the steep learning curve.
